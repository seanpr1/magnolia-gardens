## Delivery and maintenance

New website derivatives use neutral `curated-20261019-*` filenames under `assets/projects/`, with AVIF first, WebP second and progressive JPEG fallback. Landscape widths are 480/800/1200 pixels; portrait widths are 480/800/1080 pixels. A 1600-pixel island export is available only to the larger mulch-service feature; the homepage stops at 1200 pixels. The original aspect ratios, graded colors and scene content are preserved, and identifying metadata is removed from the new derivatives.

Only the lead photograph loads eagerly. Supporting photographs load lazily with explicit aspect-ratio dimensions, responsive `sizes` and `srcset`. The homepage lightbox requests the larger image when opened and supplies a descriptive caption. AVIF is included in the build's public-asset allowlist.
