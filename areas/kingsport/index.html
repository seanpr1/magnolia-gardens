What Kingsport lawns need

Kingsport sits in the ridge-and-valley country of upper East Tennessee, and the ground here is mostly heavy clay. Clay holds water after a storm and bakes hard in a July dry spell. Tall fescue is the standard lawn grass, and it does best cut high and cut on a regular schedule. We set blade height by season and vary the mowing pattern so the turf does not rut. On the steeper lots around Ridgefields and the Bays Mountain foothills, we work drainage lines carefully and stay off wet ground.

What we take care of

Every visit covers mowing, string trimming around beds and obstacles, clean edges along drives and walks, and a full blow-off of hard surfaces before we leave. Brush and overgrowth clearing and spring or fall cleanups are quoted on their own. We also add mulch, bed weeding, and small-shrub pruning when a property calls for it. What we do not do is chemical treatment or fertilization - for that we will point you to a licensed applicator. We mow; we don't spray.
