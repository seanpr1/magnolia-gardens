Frequently asked

The questions we get before the first visit.

Straight answers. If yours isn't here, text us at 423-390-9954 — we'll add it.

What areas do you serve?

Primary service area is Kingsport — zip codes 37660, 37663, 37664, and 37665. We also run set route days into Johnson City and the Tennessee side of Bristol (37620 and 37625) from our Kingsport base. Other Sullivan County addresses are case-by-case. Grouping stops by area is how we keep schedules predictable.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Magnolia Gardens Landscaping, LLC is a registered Tennessee LLC with an active Sullivan County business license. General liability insurance is in place through Acuity in the LLC's name.

What's included in a standard mowing visit?

Basic Lawn Visit: mowing, string trimming around obstacles, and blowing clippings off all hard surfaces (driveway, walkways, patio).

Full Lawn Service: everything in Basic, plus hard edges along driveway and sidewalks, bed edges, and a post-service walkthrough.

How is pricing set?

Standard residential mowing typically runs $49–$125 per visit depending on lot size and service level. Brush clearing, overgrowth, seasonal cleanups, and properties over an acre are quoted individually after a quick look at photos or a walk-through.

Recurring schedules earn a per-visit discount: 15% off weekly, 10% off biweekly. One-time visits run a 20% premium — a one-off job has the same setup and drive time as a recurring visit with no future revenue.

What payment methods do you accept?

Zelle, Venmo, cash, and check. Card payment via Stripe is coming. No card surcharges — you don't pay extra for the convenience of paying.

What happens if it rains?

If conditions make the work unsafe or harmful to the lawn — sustained heavy rain, lightning, or frozen ground — we reschedule. You'll get the text the moment we know, not the morning of.

Do I have to be home?

No. As long as we can access the yard (gate code or unlocked, dogs sorted), we work whether you're home or not. You'll get a text with photos and a summary when the visit's done.

What's the cancellation policy?

Recurring service: 14 days' written notice (a text counts). One-time visits: 24 hours.

What if something gets damaged during a visit?

We photograph it immediately, text you before we leave the property, and document it on our end. Damage caused during service is covered by our general liability policy per the policy's terms.

What do you not do?

We don't apply chemicals, fertilizer, or pesticides. We don't fell trees, grind stumps, repair irrigation, repair hardscape (pavers, walls, concrete), clean gutters, or remove snow. If you ask for any of those, we'll point you to someone local who does that work well.

How do I get started?

Text a few photos of your yard to 423-390-9954, or fill out the intake form above. We come back with a real quote — usually the same day — and schedule when you're ready.
