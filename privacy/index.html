Privacy

Updated October 11, 2026

This notice explains how Magnolia Gardens Landscaping uses information from this website, quote requests and job applications. For questions, email {{contact.email}} or call {{contact.phoneDisplay}}.

Quote requests and customer information

Our quote forms require a phone number. You can also provide your name, email, property address, service needs and project details. We use this information to respond, understand the work, prepare a quote and coordinate service. Photos you choose to send help us assess your property.

Forms can include the page you visited, referring page, campaign information and submission time to help us understand where requests came from. Our website also uses browser storage to remember referral and campaign information.

Formspree receives and stores our website forms. For customer quote requests, Formspree forwards the submitted details to Zapier to help create and route requests in Jobber. We use business tools, including Google Workspace, Jobber and QuickBooks, for communication, customer work and accounting. Information relevant to those tasks may be held in those systems.

Job applications

Our application form collects the contact information, availability, work experience and other answers you choose to provide. We use them to review applications and contact candidates. Formspree handles the application submission.

We keep application records for hiring and related recordkeeping needs. Records may be archived or deleted when appropriate; archiving does not delete them. To ask about your application or request deletion, contact us using the details above. Please do not include Social Security numbers, bank details or copies of identification in a website application. If hired, separate onboarding paperwork covers those details.

Website measurement and advertising

Our public website uses Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity and the Meta Pixel to understand visits, interactions and how people find us, and to support website and advertising improvements. These services may process device and browser information, IP addresses, page views, referral information and interactions using cookies or similar technologies.

Microsoft Clarity provides interaction measurements, heatmaps and session replay. Microsoft may also use collected information for security and advertising. Read the Microsoft Privacy Statement for its data practices.

Cloudflare provides website hosting and performance measurement. Its Web Analytics measures page performance without using analytics cookies. Other tools described above have their own data practices.

Provider information: Zapier’s data privacy information, Google Workspace privacy and security, Jobber’s privacy policy, Intuit’s privacy information, Google’s partner-site data use, Meta’s privacy policy, Cloudflare’s privacy policy and Formspree’s privacy policy.

Your choices and questions

You can call or text us instead of using a website form. Reply STOP to opt out of texts. Browser settings and privacy tools can limit cookies and tracking; their effect varies by browser and provider.

Contact us to ask about information you have provided or to request a correction or deletion. We will review the request, including any records needed for ongoing services, accounting or legal obligations. Please avoid sending sensitive identification documents with an initial privacy inquiry.
