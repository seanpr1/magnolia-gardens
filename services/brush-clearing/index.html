When a yard gets ahead of you

Life gets busy, a rental sits between tenants, or a back corner just never gets touched - and suddenly there's waist-high grass, brush on the bank, and fence lines you can't see. We specialize in resetting exactly those areas, getting overgrown ground back to something you can mow and maintain normally again.

Fence lines, banks, and back lots

We clear tall grass and weeds, cut back brush along fence lines and property edges, and tackle the steeper banks and back lots that a standard mow won't touch. On the slopes common around Ridgefields and the Bays Mountain foothills, we work carefully and stay off ground that's too wet to be safe. Everything we cut gets hauled away.

How is brush clearing priced?

Every brush job is different, so these are quoted individually after we see photos - the price depends on how heavy the growth is, the size of the area, and access. There is no flat rate, but the quote is clear and fixed before we start.

Do you clear fence lines, banks, and back lots?

Yes - tall grass, overgrown fence lines, brush along banks, and back corners that have gotten out of hand. We cut it down, clear it out, and leave the area usable again.

Do you haul off what you cut?

Yes, haul-off is part of the job. We do not leave piles behind unless you ask us to keep them.

Can you clear a lot before a property sale or for code compliance?

That is a common reason people call. We can knock back an overgrown lot quickly so it shows well or meets a city or HOA notice. For upkeep afterward, a maintenance plan keeps it from coming back.
